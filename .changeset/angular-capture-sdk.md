Add `@crikket-io/capture-angular`, an Angular integration for the capture SDK. Ships `provideCrikket()`, `CrikketModule.forRoot()` and an injectable `CrikketService` that lazy-loads `@crikket-io/capture` (SSR-safe), with an `enabled` flag to keep the widget out of production.
